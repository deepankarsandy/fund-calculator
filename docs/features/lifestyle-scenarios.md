# Lifestyle Scenarios & 1-Click Plan Switching

The calculator provides real-time sensitivity analysis across three lifestyle tiers and allows instant 1-click plan switching directly from the results page.

---

## Overview

Retirement readiness is not a binary yes/no; it depends on lifestyle flexibility. Users can compare how much capital they need to survive comfortably versus living frugally or luxuriously, and immediately toggle their active plan between these tiers.

---

## The Three Lifestyle Tiers

| Tier | Name | Composition | Multiplier / Logic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | **Frugal / Bare Minimum** | Survival essentials only: shelter, groceries, basic utilities, and vital healthcare. | Discretionary items = ₹0; parent lump sums = 0.65×. |
| **Tier 2** | **Comfortable** | Balanced lifestyle with leisure, occasional dining, hobbies, and vacation buffer. | Standard baseline (1.0×). |
| **Tier 3** | **Luxury / Aspirational** | Elevated lifestyle with premium housing, fine dining, frequent travel, and superior healthcare. | Discretionary items = 1.5×; core essentials = 1.15×; parent lump sums = 1.4×. |

---

## Key Features

### 1. Side-by-Side Comparison Grid
The results view presents a 3-card comparison grid summarizing for each lifestyle:
- **Monthly Budget (Today)**
- **Total Lifetime Spend**
- **Corpus Target Needed**
- **Projected Peak Accumulation**

### 2. Interactive 1-Click Plan Switching
- Every card in the lifestyle section is a clickable button.
- Clicking any card immediately applies that lifestyle to your financial plan:
  - Recalculates all mathematical targets, savings accumulation, and year-by-year projections.
  - Updates the top Hero card, readiness score, and charts in real-time.
  - Shows the **“Active Plan”** badge on the selected card.

### 3. Preserved Expense Structure (Non-Destructive)
- Switching between lifestyle plans **does not delete or remove any expense fields**.
- Under Bare Minimum, discretionary items have their amounts set to ₹0 while remaining visible.
- Switching to Comfortable or Luxury instantly restores or scales the budget without losing user-customized numbers.
