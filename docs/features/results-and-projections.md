# Results Dashboard & Projections

The results dashboard provides an end-to-end visual and tabular breakdown of retirement readiness, lifetime cash flows, portfolio trajectories, and funding gaps.

---

## Overview

Step 6 consolidates the financial model into an actionable dashboard that answers the core question: *“Am I on track, and will my savings last my lifetime?”*

---

## Key Dashboard Components

### 1. Retirement Readiness Hero Card
- **Target Corpus Needed ($C_R$)**: The exact mathematical capital required at retirement age to fund expenses through your target age.
- **Projected Accumulated Corpus ($P_R$)**: Your projected portfolio balance accumulated from career savings, salary increments, and compounding returns.
- **Readiness Score Progress Bar**:
  - $\ge 100\%$: **Fully Funded** (Emerald) with displayed surplus capital.
  - $70\% - 99\%$: **Moderate Shortfall** (Amber).
  - $< 70\%$: **Critical Shortfall** (Rose) displaying extra starting capital needed or depletion age.

### 2. Inflow, Outflow & Compounding Cards
Three high-contrast summary cards:
- **Inflows & Savings (+)**: Monthly take-home pay today, career surplus invested, and starting liquid capital.
- **Living Outflows (-)**: Current monthly burn rate, annual spend today, and total cumulative inflated lifetime living costs.
- **Wealth Compounding (+)**: Total investment returns generated over lifetime, projected peak wealth, and ending balance at age 85+.

### 3. Lifetime Cashflow Reconciliation Ledger
A transparent 3-pillar ledger accounting for every single rupee:
1. **Total Lifetime Outflows**: Cumulative inflated expenses spent across the planning horizon.
2. **Total Inflows & Investment Returns**: Total career savings + initial liquid assets + lifetime compound growth.
3. **Net Ending Wealth**: Inflows minus outflows, explaining generational legacy wealth or capital exhaustion.

### 4. Interactive Data Visualizations (Recharts)
Four interactive tabbed views:
- **Income vs Expenses Crossover**: Visualizes annual earned salary vs inflating living costs over time. Pinpoints the retirement crossover point where salary drops to zero and portfolio drawdown commences.
- **Wealth Timeline (Corpus Curve)**: Depicts portfolio accumulation up to retirement age, peak corpus, and subsequent drawdown across Optimistic, Base, and Conservative market regimes.
- **Expense Category Pie Chart**: Visual breakdown of current living costs across the 11 categories.
- **Year-by-Year Cashflow Table**: Detailed paginated ledger displaying exact figures for age, salary, living costs, net savings invested, net withdrawals, investment returns, and closing portfolio balance for every planning year.
