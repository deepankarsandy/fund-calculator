# Release v0.3.0

**Release Version:** `0.3.0`  
**Previous Version:** `0.2.0`  
**Release Date:** In Development (November 2026)

Release v0.3.0 focuses on state preservation, data safety guarantees, interactive confirmation workflows, and bug fixes across the Lifetime Cost & Corpus Calculator suite. This document tracks changes and will expand as additional improvements and fixes are introduced.

---

## What's New & In Progress in v0.3.0

### 1. User Profile Defaults Synchronization & Data Safety Fix

- **Household Dependents Preservation**:
  - **Issue Resolved**: Previously, saving profile defaults in the User Profile modal or page with "Auto-apply to all 5 calculators" checked reset the household members list in the Retirement Fund Planner (`useScenarioStore`) to `members: [selfMember]`, inadvertently deleting all entered dependents (e.g., spouse, children, parents).
  - **Fix**: `applyProfileDefaults` now strictly preserves all existing household members and dependents. Only the primary `self` member's age and label are updated to match the profile demographics, keeping all entered family members intact.

- **Entered Expenses & Custom Budgets Protection**:
  - **Issue Resolved**: Previously, applying profile defaults completely regenerated all expense categories via `buildCategoriesForProfile(...)`, wiping out user-entered expense amounts (such as mobile recharge, broadband, rent, groceries) and restoring them to baseline system defaults.
  - **Fix**: All existing categories, expense items, entered amounts, custom items, notes, and skipped statuses are preserved. Only the category `defaultInflationRate` is updated when sector-specific inflation is configured in the profile.

- **Selective Parameter Synchronization**:
  - Profile default application now strictly updates only the specific values configured in the profile:
    - **Retirement Age**: Synchronized to `assets.incomeEndAge`.
    - **Current Age & DOB**: Synchronized to `profile.currentAge` and self member age.
    - **Income & Wealth**: Synchronized to `assets.monthlyIncome`, `assets.incomeGrowthRate`, and `assets.existingInvestableCorpus`.
    - **Inflation Parameters**: Synchronized to `assumptions.globalInflationRate` and `assumptions.inflationMode` (`simple` vs. `category`).
    - **Expected Return Expectations**: Baseline return expectations updated across planners and helper calculators.
    - **Demographic Baselines**: Target life expectancy, location cost multiplier, and lifestyle baseline.
  - **Automatic Live Re-computation**: If retirement projections were already computed, they are automatically recalculated with the new parameters so results stay current without needing manual re-triggering.

### 4. Indian Retirement Calibration: NPS, EPF & Custom Insurance Plans

- **NPS Auto-Filling in Income & Savings (`otherIncome`)**:
  - **Indian PFRDA Rules & Market Rates**:
    - Accumulation models historical blended Tier-1 returns of **10.0% p.a.** across Scheme E (Equity), C (Corporate Bonds), and G (Government Securities) over working years ($N = \text{retirementAge} - \text{currentAge}$).
    - Applies statutory exit rule: minimum **40% of accumulated corpus** is converted into an immediate annuity plan.
    - Applies prevailing Indian Annuity Service Provider (ASP) rate of **6.0% p.a.** (LIC, HDFC Life, SBI Life, ICICI Prudential) for lifelong pension.
    - Starting timing: Payout begins strictly **after set retirement age** (Planning Year $N + 1$).
    - Also auto-populates the statutory **60% tax-free lump sum** in the Future Lump Sums section at retirement year $N$.
- **EPF / PF Auto-Filling in Lump Sum Section (`futureContributions`)**:
  - **Official EPFO Rules & Rates**:
    - Computes cumulative compound balance at the official Central Board of Trustees (CBT) interest rate of **8.25% p.a.** (notified for FY 2023-24, FY 2024-25, and FY 2025-26).
    - Compounds monthly contributions (Employee + Employer share) over active career years.
    - Sets full 100% tax-free lump sum maturity at retirement year $N$.
- **Custom Insurance Plans as Regular Income Source**:
  - **Starting from Current Year**:
    - Unlike post-retirement pensions, custom insurance-linked retirement plans (e.g., Guaranteed Income Life Plans, Money-Back Policies, Deferred Annuities, Child Education Plans) can pay regular annual income **starting in the current year** ($t = 1$, at current age).
    - Cash inflows received during working years contribute to annual savings surplus and compound into the retirement corpus; payouts extending into retirement directly offset living expenses.
  - **UI & UX Enhancements**:
    - Added dedicated **`+ Add Insurance Plan (Starts Current Year)`** quick-action button in Step 2 ("Income, Savings & Assets").
    - Added `incomeType` dropdown (`insurance_plan`, `nps_annuity`, `pension`, `rental`, `other`).
    - Added clear badge indicators: `● Starts in Current Year` (green) vs `Starts in Year X` (blue), and `🛡️ Insurance Policy` vs `🏛️ NPS Annuity` vs `EPFO 8.25%`.
    - Added contextual calibration callout banner in Step 2 explaining Indian EPF, NPS, and insurance plan modeling.
- **Data Safety & Non-Destructive Merging**:
  - User-entered custom income sources (such as existing rental properties or custom insurance policies) and custom lump sums (such as ancestral land sales or inheritances) are strictly preserved whenever profile defaults are applied or refreshed.

### 5. Linked SIP, Fixed Deposit (FD) & Loan EMI Inflow/Liability Integration

- **Retirement Planner Income Section Integration (SIP & FD)**:
  - **Clean, Compact Summary Cards**: Rather than bloating the retirement wizard with complex multi-year input schedules, data is pre-filled directly from the user's dedicated SIP and Fixed Deposit calculators.
  - **All SIP Inflow Types Supported**:
    - **Regular SIP (Wealth Accumulation)**: Pre-fills net post-tax maturity corpus as a Future Lump Sum cash inflow in the target maturity year $N$.
    - **SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan)**: Pre-fills regular monthly/annual cash withdrawal payouts into Other Income from Year 1 to Year $N$, directly reducing the retirement savings burden.
    - **SIP-to-SWP Combo Plan**: Accurately phases cashflows — Phase 1 accumulation during working years, followed by Phase 2 regular monthly pension payouts commencing post-SIP accumulation.
  - **All Fixed Deposit (FD) Payout Types Supported**:
    - **Cumulative FD**: Compounded interest and principal pre-filled as a Future Lump Sum maturity in the FD maturity year.
    - **Monthly Payout FD**: Monthly interest credited as regular recurring income across the tenure, with full principal repayment pre-filled at the tenure end.
    - **Quarterly Payout FD**: Quarterly interest payouts credited into regular income, with full principal returned at maturity.
  - **Interactive Controls & Exclusion Toggle**:
    - Dedicated **"Exclude from Retirement Calc" / "Include in Retirement Calc"** button to toggle items in or out of retirement calculations at will.
    - Prominent **"Edit in SIP Calculator ↗"** and **"Edit in Fixed Deposit Calculator ↗"** deep links for adjusting parameters without cluttering the retirement form.
    - Selector tabs/pills to easily switch between or link any variant (Regular SIP vs. SWP vs. Combo, Cumulative vs. Monthly vs. Quarterly).

- **Retirement Planner Expenses Section Integration (Loan EMI)**:
  - **Fixed 0% Inflation Contractual Liabilities**: Unlike standard living expenses that inflate over time, loan repayments are contractual and fixed nominal obligations.
  - **All Loan Types Supported**:
    - **Home Loan**: 20-year reducing balance mortgage obligation.
    - **Car Loan**: 5-year auto loan EMI.
    - **Personal Loan**: 3-year short-term loan obligation.
    - **Custom Loan**: Direct sync of user-customized principal, interest rate, tenure, and prepayment adjustments from the Loan EMI Calculator.
  - **Payoff Horizon & Automatic Drop-Off**: Pre-filled into the Living Expenses model for the loan's exact payoff duration (Years 1 to $N$), dropping to zero once the loan is fully amortized.
  - **Interactive Controls & Exclusion Toggle**:
    - One-click **"Exclude from Retirement Calc" / "Include in Retirement Calc"** toggle.
    - **"Edit in Loan EMI Calculator ↗"** deep link for testing prepayment schedules, prepayments, and interest rate adjustments.

- **Data Protection & Anti-Clutter Design**:
  - Filtered view in manual form fields ensures that system-linked items do not bloat the manual array rows in Step 2 or category lists in Step 4.
  - Non-destructive merging ensures custom user incomes, lump sums, and expense categories are 100% preserved.

### 6. Default Household Dependents Profile Tab & Retirement Calculator Auto-Sync

- **Dedicated Dependents Tab in User Profile**:
  - Added a new **"Dependents"** tab to both the quick-access User Profile modal (`UserProfileModal.tsx`) and the full-page profile manager (`/profile`).
  - Allows users to configure their family members once as part of their global baseline profile, eliminating repetitive entry across sessions.
  - Supports all household relationships: Spouse, Children, Elderly Parents, Siblings, and Other Dependents.
  - Configurable parameters per dependent:
    - **Name / Label**: Nickname or identifier (e.g., "Priya", "First Child", "Father").
    - **Relationship**: Role within the household (`spouse`, `child`, `parent`, `sibling`, `other`).
    - **Current Age**: Validated integer age (0 to 120).
    - **Support Until Age**: Optional age when financial support ends (e.g., 25 for a child graduating college; lifelong if omitted).
    - **Financially Dependent**: Indicates whether this member relies on the user's corpus for living expenses.
    - **Health Insurance**: Tracks dedicated or family floater health insurance coverage.

- **Intelligent Quick-Add Presets & Visual Indicators**:
  - One-click quick-add buttons for instant setup:
    - **`+ Spouse`**: Calibrated to user age with active health coverage.
    - **`+ Child`**: Pre-calibrated with an education support horizon up to age 25.
    - **`+ Parent`**: Pre-calibrated with parental support horizon up to age 85.
    - **`+ Other`**: Generic dependent configuration.
  - Summary badges track active education horizons (children) and care horizons (parents).

- **Retirement Calculator Auto-Sync & Expense Activation**:
  - When profile defaults are applied (via profile save with "Auto-apply to all 5 calculators" or the **"Auto-set from Profile"** button in the retirement wizard), default dependents are automatically synchronized to **Step 3 (Household Members)**.
  - **Smart Category Activation**: Automatically activates child schooling fees, higher education / college costs, and parental support expenses in the Living Expenses model (Category H: Family & Dependents) with calibrated start and end year time-windows.
  - **Non-Destructive State Merging**: Strictly preserves any existing user-entered dependents or customized expense amounts while merging profile defaults.
  - Full **Export & Import JSON** portability and schema validation for profile dependents.
