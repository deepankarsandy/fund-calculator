# Release v0.3.0

**Release Version:** `0.3.0`  
**Previous Version:** `0.2.0`  
**Release Date:** In Development (November 2026)

Release v0.3.0 focuses on state preservation, data safety guarantees, interactive confirmation workflows, and bug fixes across the Lifetime Cost & Corpus Calculator suite. This document tracks changes and will expand as additional improvements and fixes are introduced.

---

## What's New & In Progress in v0.3.0

### 1. User Profile Defaults Synchronization & Data Safety Fix

- **Household Dependents Preservation**:
  - **Issue Resolved**: Previously, saving profile defaults in the User Profile modal or page with "Auto-apply to all 5 calculators" checked reset the household members list in the Retirement Fund Planner (`useScenarioStore`) to `members: [selfMember]`, inadvertently deleting all entered dependents (e.g., spouse, children, parents).
  - **Fix**: `applyProfileDefaults` now strictly preserves all existing household members and dependents. Only the primary `self` member's age and label are updated to match the profile demographics, keeping all entered family members intact.

- **Entered Expenses & Custom Budgets Protection**:
  - **Issue Resolved**: Previously, applying profile defaults completely regenerated all expense categories via `buildCategoriesForProfile(...)`, wiping out user-entered expense amounts (such as mobile recharge, broadband, rent, groceries) and restoring them to baseline system defaults.
  - **Fix**: All existing categories, expense items, entered amounts, custom items, notes, and skipped statuses are preserved. Only the category `defaultInflationRate` is updated when sector-specific inflation is configured in the profile.

- **Selective Parameter Synchronization**:
  - Profile default application now strictly updates only the specific values configured in the profile:
    - **Retirement Age**: Synchronized to `assets.incomeEndAge`.
    - **Current Age & DOB**: Synchronized to `profile.currentAge` and self member age.
    - **Income & Wealth**: Synchronized to `assets.monthlyIncome`, `assets.incomeGrowthRate`, and `assets.existingInvestableCorpus`.
    - **Inflation Parameters**: Synchronized to `assumptions.globalInflationRate` and `assumptions.inflationMode` (`simple` vs. `category`).
    - **Expected Return Expectations**: Baseline return expectations updated across planners and helper calculators.
    - **Demographic Baselines**: Target life expectancy, location cost multiplier, and lifestyle baseline.
  - **Automatic Live Re-computation**: If retirement projections were already computed, they are automatically recalculated with the new parameters so results stay current without needing manual re-triggering.

### 2. Profile Sync Confirmation Modal Dialog

- **Interactive Pre-Save Confirmation**:
  - Added a dedicated confirmation dialog (`ProfileSyncConfirmModal.tsx`) when saving profile defaults with the **"Auto-apply to all 5 calculators"** checkbox enabled.
  - Implemented across both the dedicated Profile page (`/profile`) and the quick-access Profile drawer modal (`UserProfileModal.tsx`).
- **Clear Impact Breakdown**:
  - **What Will Update**: Explains that retirement age, current age, calculation mode, inflation rates, expected return baselines, and tax slabs will sync to profile values.
  - **What Will NOT Change (Protected)**: Reassures the user with a safety guarantee that custom entered expense amounts (e.g. mobile recharge, food, rent) and household dependents (spouse, children, parents) will remain untouched.
  - **Bypass on Direct Save**: If the "Auto-apply" checkbox is unchecked, changes save directly to the profile without triggering the modal prompt.

### 4. Indian Retirement Calibration: NPS, EPF & Custom Insurance Plans

- **NPS Auto-Filling in Income & Savings (`otherIncome`)**:
  - **Indian PFRDA Rules & Market Rates**:
    - Accumulation models historical blended Tier-1 returns of **10.0% p.a.** across Scheme E (Equity), C (Corporate Bonds), and G (Government Securities) over working years ($N = \text{retirementAge} - \text{currentAge}$).
    - Applies statutory exit rule: minimum **40% of accumulated corpus** is converted into an immediate annuity plan.
    - Applies prevailing Indian Annuity Service Provider (ASP) rate of **6.0% p.a.** (LIC, HDFC Life, SBI Life, ICICI Prudential) for lifelong pension.
    - Starting timing: Payout begins strictly **after set retirement age** (Planning Year $N + 1$).
    - Also auto-populates the statutory **60% tax-free lump sum** in the Future Lump Sums section at retirement year $N$.
- **EPF / PF Auto-Filling in Lump Sum Section (`futureContributions`)**:
  - **Official EPFO Rules & Rates**:
    - Computes cumulative compound balance at the official Central Board of Trustees (CBT) interest rate of **8.25% p.a.** (notified for FY 2023-24, FY 2024-25, and FY 2025-26).
    - Compounds monthly contributions (Employee + Employer share) over active career years.
    - Sets full 100% tax-free lump sum maturity at retirement year $N$.
- **Custom Insurance Plans as Regular Income Source**:
  - **Starting from Current Year**:
    - Unlike post-retirement pensions, custom insurance-linked retirement plans (e.g., Guaranteed Income Life Plans, Money-Back Policies, Deferred Annuities, Child Education Plans) can pay regular annual income **starting in the current year** ($t = 1$, at current age).
    - Cash inflows received during working years contribute to annual savings surplus and compound into the retirement corpus; payouts extending into retirement directly offset living expenses.
  - **UI & UX Enhancements**:
    - Added dedicated **`+ Add Insurance Plan (Starts Current Year)`** quick-action button in Step 2 ("Income, Savings & Assets").
    - Added `incomeType` dropdown (`insurance_plan`, `nps_annuity`, `pension`, `rental`, `other`).
    - Added clear badge indicators: `● Starts in Current Year` (green) vs `Starts in Year X` (blue), and `🛡️ Insurance Policy` vs `🏛️ NPS Annuity` vs `EPFO 8.25%`.
    - Added contextual calibration callout banner in Step 2 explaining Indian EPF, NPS, and insurance plan modeling.
- **Data Safety & Non-Destructive Merging**:
  - User-entered custom income sources (such as existing rental properties or custom insurance policies) and custom lump sums (such as ancestral land sales or inheritances) are strictly preserved whenever profile defaults are applied or refreshed.
