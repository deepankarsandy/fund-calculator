## Detailed Breakdown of Changes

### 1. Public Provident Fund (PPF) Calculator & Retirement Fund Integration

#### A. Comprehensive PPF Calculation Engine (`src/domain/calculations/ppf.ts`)

- **Indian Sovereign Rules & Exempt-Exempt-Exempt (EEE) Regime**:
  - Implements the complete statutory rules of the Government of India Public Provident Fund Scheme (1968 / 2019):
    - **15-Year Mandatory Lock-In**: Models the full 15-financial-year initial investment cycle.
    - **Unlimited 5-Year Extension Blocks**: Models extension blocks beyond 15 years up to 50 years, supporting both:
      - _With Further Contributions_ (active compounding + continuing annual investments).
      - _Without Further Contributions_ (corpus continues compounding at prevailing interest rates with zero new deposits).
    - **Government Notified Interest Rate**: Baseline configured to the official government-notified rate of **7.1% p.a.** (compounded annually on balances between the 5th and end of each month).
    - **Yearly vs. Monthly Contribution Schedules**:
      - _Yearly Deposit_: Models lump sum deposited before April 5th to maximize annual interest accrual across the full 12 months.
      - _Monthly Deposit_: Precisely models the Indian monthly 5th rule ($P_0 + 6.5 \times M$) for accurate annual interest accruals.
    - **Statutory Contribution Bounds**: Enforces official limits of **₹500 minimum** and **₹1,50,000 maximum** per financial year.
    - **Section 80C Tax Deduction Tracking**: Tracks tax saved each year up to ₹1.5 Lakh based on the user's applicable tax slab.
    - **Triple Tax Exemption (EEE)**: Interest accrued and maturity payouts are 100% tax-free under Section 10(11) of the Income Tax Act.
    - **Existing PPF Account Tracking**: Allows users with active PPF accounts to enter their current balance and years already elapsed (up to 29 years), accurately forecasting remaining projection years and future maturity.
    - **Real Purchasing Power**: Calculates inflation-adjusted present value of the final maturity corpus using projected inflation rates.

#### B. User Interface & Experience (`src/components/calculator/PpfCalculatorSection.tsx` & `src/pages/FdCalculatorPage.tsx`)

- **Integrated Mode Switcher**:
  - Added a responsive tabbed switcher on `/fd` ("Fixed Deposit (FD) & PPF") allowing users to toggle between Bank Fixed Deposits and Public Provident Fund calculations.
- **Interactive Controls & Presets**:
  - Quick-preset deposit buttons (₹50,000, ₹1,00,000, ₹1,50,000 max statutory limit).
  - Quick-preset interest rates (7.1% govt notified, 7.5%, 8.0%).
  - Extension block selector (15 years up to 50 years in 5-year increments).
  - Summary KPI cards: _Total Maturity Corpus (Tax-Free)_, _Total Principal Deposited_, _Total Interest Earned_, _Total 80C Tax Saved_, and _Inflation-Adjusted Purchasing Power_.
  - Full year-by-year amortized schedule table with opening balance, deposits, interest earned, closing balance, and 80C tax deductions.
  - "Apply Profile Defaults" button syncing tax slabs and inflation assumptions directly from the user's profile.
  - Dedicated local storage persistence (`src/lib/ppfStorage.ts`) and active tab state caching.

#### C. Retirement Fund Planner Integration (`src/components/wizard/LinkedIncomeCalculatorsCard.tsx`)

- **Standalone `LinkedPpfInflowCard`**:
  - Added a dedicated PPF inflow card to Step 2 ("Income, Savings & Assets" -> Linked Helper Inflows) alongside SIP and FD cards.
  - **One-Click Include / Exclude**: Users can toggle whether their PPF maturity corpus is included in retirement projections.
  - **Automatic Future Lump Sum Cash Inflow**: Injects the net tax-free maturity corpus into the retirement scenario at Year `tenureYears` as a future contribution (`futureContributions`).
  - **Deep-Link Navigation**: Features an "Edit in PPF Calculator" quick action to jump directly to the PPF setup tab with immediate synchronization.
  - **JSON Import / Export Integration**: Updated `linkedCalculatorsStorage.ts` and `exportImportStorage.ts` to include PPF configurations in full backup and restore workflows.

### 2. Bug Fix: Number Input Field (Clear and Type Values)

- **Root Cause & Previous Problem**:
  - Previously, numeric fields implemented using standard HTML `<input type="number">` or controlled React state immediately clamped or reset to `0` / minimum value whenever a user backspaced or cleared the field.
  - This made it impossible to delete digits to type a new number without the input jumping or prepending zeros.
- **Solution (`src/components/ui/NumberInput.tsx`)**:
  - Built an internal string-buffered `NumberInput` component.
  - Maintains a local string state (`localText`) that tracks intermediate user keystrokes without prematurely triggering resets.
  - Users can now select all, backspace completely (`""`), or edit numbers naturally.
  - Validates and parses numbers on typing, while clamping to `min`, `max`, or falling back to `fallbackValue` on `blur`.
  - Supports `integerOnly`, `allowEmpty`, `step`, and custom change callbacks.
- **Application-Wide Adoption**:
  - Replaced native inputs across:
    - `DynamicExpensesRenderer` (monthly amounts, expense windows, inflation overrides).
    - `ProfilePage` & `UserProfileModal` (ages, incomes, growth rates, assets, tax slabs).
    - `PpfCalculatorSection` (annual/monthly deposits, interest rate, tenure, tax slab).
    - `FdCalculatorPage` (principal amount, interest rate, tenure years/months).
    - `InvestmentCalculatorPage` & `CustomPlanCalculator` (initial investment, time horizons, asset return rates).
    - `LoanEmiCalculatorPage` & `SipCalculatorPage` (loan principal, EMIs, SIP amounts).
    - `ProfileDependentsTab` & `DynamicField`.
- **Unit Test Coverage (`src/components/ui/__tests__/NumberInput.test.tsx`)**:
  - 9 test cases verifying clearing, typing, blur handling, integer validation, min/max clamping, and fallback states.

### 4. Enhancement: Expense Field Types are Now Labels instead of Input Fields

- **Clean Typography Labels (`src/components/expenses/DynamicExpensesRenderer.tsx`)**:
  - Default/system expense item names (e.g., Groceries, Rent, Electricity, Fuel, Health Insurance) are now rendered as crisp, read-only typography labels (`<span className="...">`) instead of editable text input boxes.
  - Eliminates visual clutter and prevents accidental edits to canonical expense names.
- **Editable Custom Expenses**:
  - Added `isCustom?: boolean` to the `ExpenseItem` domain model (`src/domain/models/expense.ts`).
